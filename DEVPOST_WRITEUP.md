## Tagline
One Step turns an overwhelming task into small steps and shows you just one at a time.

## Inspiration
Big tasks can feel impossible to start, especially for people with ADHD, dyslexia or anxiety. Most productivity tools show long lists, which makes that worse. We wanted a tool that shows less, not more.

## What it does
You type a task, such as "Finish my assignment". One Step splits it into 3–6 small steps and shows only the current one, with a suggested time and a gentle focus timer. You tick it off, the next step appears, and a progress bar shows how far you have come. You can add, remove or postpone steps.

## Accessibility (the heart of the project)
- Full keyboard use, skip link, strong focus outlines, screen reader labels and announcements
- Text size up to 150%, easy-read font, extra line spacing
- Light, dark and high-contrast colours; reduce-motion option; follows system preferences
- Read each step aloud, and speak your task
- Soft chime instead of a harsh alarm; timer is optional
- English and Urdu, including right-to-left layout
- Private by design: everything is stored in the browser

## How we built it
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no backend. Steps are generated by a rule-based template engine so it works offline. Voice uses the browser's Web Speech API. Progress is saved in localStorage.
